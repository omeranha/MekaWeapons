# Mekanism: Weapons

**Mekanism: Weapons** is an unofficial addon for [Mekanism](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mekanism), introducing advanced, highly customizable weapons that blend seamlessly with the iconic "Meka" item aesthetic.

## Weapons

- **Meka-Tana**: A high-tech katana capable of dealing massive damage and teleporting the player.
- **Meka-Bow**: An advanced bow that elevates ranged combat with unique features and modular upgrades.
- **Meka-Gun**: A powerful laser gun that fires precise energy lasers, offering a futuristic alternative to traditional firearms.

Every weapon is installed and configured at the Modification Station, like Mekanism's own gear, and supports the
Energy Unit and the Color Modulation Unit.

## Shared Modules

- **Weapon Attack Amplification Unit**: Increases damage up to 4x (configurable).
- **Looting Unit**: Functions like the Looting enchantment, up to level 5.

## Meka-Bow Modules

- **Auto Fire**: Enables continuous, automatic arrow shooting.
- **Draw Speed**: Reduces the time required to fully draw the bow for quicker shots, without losing shot power.
- **Gravity Dampener**: Modifies arrow trajectory for improved accuracy and range, allowing arrows to fly straight.
- **Energy Arrows**: Fires arrows made of pure energy, removing the need for ammunition.
- **Punch**: Knocks targets back further.
- **Chemical Arrows**: Adds a chemical tank to the bow and infuses the arrows with the stored chemical. Fill it from
  any Mekanism chemical tank or pipe:
  - **Hydrogen**: sets the target on fire.
  - **Sulfuric Acid**: poisons the target.
  - **Hydrogen Chloride**: weakens the target.
  - **Water Vapor**: slows the target.

## Meka-Tana Modules

- **Sweeping**: Grants sweeping attacks, hitting multiple targets at once.
- **Teleportation**: Allows the player to teleport short distances, adding mobility and tactical options.
- **Fire Aspect**: Sets the targets struck on fire.
- **Knockback**: Knocks targets back further.

## Meka-Gun Modules

- **Cooling**: Vents heat faster and shortens the cooldown delay, at the cost of extra energy per shot.

## Additional Features

- **Wireless Charger**:
  - Charges the items in your inventory, armor and off-hand slots.
  - When equipped in a Curios slot, visually displays the weapons on your character's back for added personalization.
- **Mod compatibility**: the Meka-Tana ships with [Better Combat](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/better-combat-by-daedelus)
  and [Epic Fight](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/epic-fight-mod) attributes out of the box, so it uses
  proper katana animations and movesets when either mod is installed.

## Customization & Expansion

- Every damage value, energy cost, capacity and module effect is configurable, including how many copies of each module
  a weapon accepts (`mekaweapons-startup.toml`).
- The mod is designed for extensibility: new weapons, equipment, and modules (including custom or enchantment-based modules) can be added.
- Suggestions and contributions are welcome! Feel free to open an issue or pull request on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/omeranha/MekaWeapons).
